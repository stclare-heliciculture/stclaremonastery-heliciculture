How Snail Meat Supports Your Blood

The human body depends on a steady supply of iron, Vitamin B12, and high-quality protein to build and maintain healthy red blood cells. Snail meat delivers all three in a naturally lean package.

Iron helps form hemoglobin — the protein that carries oxygen through your bloodstream. Vitamin B12 supports the normal production of red blood cells and protects nerve health. Together, they help maintain steady energy, healthy circulation, and a body that feels strong every day.
