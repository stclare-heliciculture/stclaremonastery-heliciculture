Witness of the Quiet Hours

Long before the world wakes, the monastery is already at prayer. Then the work begins — patient, deliberate, reverent. Our snails are fed, tended, and watched over with the same faithfulness we offer to God.

We believe that whatever your hands find to do, do it with all your heart — and so we do. This sanctuary is not a business hidden from our calling; it is our calling, made visible.

“Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord.” Colossians 3:23

A Sanctuary That Serves You

What you have seen is only a glimpse. This monastery is a working ministry of provision — and its harvest is for you. Order fresh, live, or frozen snails, and let the labour of our hands serve your table with grace.
