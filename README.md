## 🐌 Who We Are

**St. Clare Monastery Heliciculture** is a ministry of sustainable snail farming, rooted in the quiet discipline of monastic life. We raise premium **live and frozen snails** for families, restaurants, markets, and exporters across Nigeria — combining faith, patience, and agricultural excellence.

Every snail is raised in a clean, ethical, and sustainable environment. No shortcuts. No compromises. Just honest work, done well.
