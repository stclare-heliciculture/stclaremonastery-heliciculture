**Premium Live & Frozen Snails — Ikoto, Ijebu-Ode, Ogun State, Nigeria**

A ministry of sustainable snail farming, rooted in faith and tradition.

---
## 🌿 About

St. Clare Monastery Heliciculture is a small, dedicated farming community in Ikoto, Ijebu-Ode, Ogun State, Nigeria. We raise snails with the same care and patience that has guided monastic life for centuries — providing premium **live and frozen snails** to families, restaurants, markets, and exporters across Nigeria.

Every snail is raised in a clean, ethical, and sustainable environment. No shortcuts, no compromises — just the quiet discipline of monastic work.
