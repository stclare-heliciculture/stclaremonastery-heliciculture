About Our Monastery

A story of quiet labour, sustainable farming,
and premium heliciculture — rooted in faith.

Where Faith Meets Sustainability

St. Clare Monastery Heliciculture is a ministry of the monastery — a small but dedicated farming community in Ikoto, Ijebu-Ode, Ogun State, Nigeria. We raise snails with the same care and patience that has guided monastic life for centuries.

What began as a humble effort to cultivate snails for local needs has grown into a trusted source of live and frozen snails for families, restaurants, markets, and exporters across Nigeria.

Every snail is raised in a clean, ethical, and sustainable environment — no shortcuts, no compromises, just the quiet discipline of monastic work.
